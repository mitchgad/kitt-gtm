# COMPANY STORY

**Signed by Mitch 2026-07-31.** Values are the four brand attributes (no separate list). No clinical-outcome claims (Truth Floor).

**Vision (10-year):** Everyone owns their health in one place, a clinician's expertise built into a companion that knows them and adapts as they go, so they stay a step ahead.

**Mission (1-3-year):** Give Australian allied-health clinicians and their clients one connected place, so a clinician's care reaches the client between sessions, not just in the room.

**Boilerplate:** kitt is clinical software for Australian allied-health clinicians, built to connect the clinician and their client and keep them both ahead. kitt Clinician turns each session's dictation into notes, letters and rehab plans, so the clinician spends more time connecting and treating; meanwhile the client carries their rehab in their pocket through kitt Companion, and their adherence and pain flow back to the clinician. Founded on Queensland's Sunshine Coast by people with over 40 years of clinical experience, kitt is building toward everyone owning and understanding their health, with their clinician's expertise beside them. Today it serves physiotherapists and exercise physiologists across Australia.

**Origin (story asset, not part of this section):** the Noosa Tri founding moment (Freya and Leigh in the water after the race, everyone staring at health data they could not make sense of). Kept as a narrative asset for the About page and founder posts.

## MARKETING ADVANTAGES

**Signed by Mitch 2026-07-31.**

## Overall Take

The real edge is the connected loop, not just a finished session: the plan reaches the client, and their adherence and pain come back to the clinician. No competitor holds both sides — scribes like Heidi stop at notes, Physitrack starts at exercises, no rival portal sends data back. The gaps are activation, not weakness: the return leg isn't customer-proven yet, the trial doesn't demo the whole loop, and the founder fuel — as content voices and as athletes carrying the branding — isn't switched on. All three advantages are nascent: opportunities, not holes.

| 1 | **The two-way record**: the only platform where a session is fully done (dictation → notes → letters → plan → in the client's hands) *and* the client's adherence and pain flow back to the clinician, keeping them connected between sessions. | Medium | High | **High** |

## Where Advantages Compound

The founders make the loop believable — a physio and an EP showing their own connected session, data coming back, beats any product tour; Freya's EP channel doubles as the EP-ICP wedge. The same two race in kitt branding, so the founder fuel pays off twice: credibility inside the content (#2), and colours, logo and imagery in front of an endurance crowd at events around the country (#3) — both riding the Ahead theme. The loop is the proof; the founders are the distribution.

## Advantages You Might Be Missing

- **Companion as an exposure loop:** every client using Companion carries kitt outside the clinic. No mechanism today, but the only structural virality candidate — pressure-test later, don't build now.
- **Trial demo of the loop (deliberately excluded):** the first note is fast, but the connected-loop moment — the actual differentiator — isn't surfaced in-product yet. Becomes a marketing advantage once the product shows it; until then it's a product note, not a marketing one.

## BRAND ATTRIBUTES (FUEL STRATEGY)

**Signed by Mitch 2026-07-30.** Every attribute traces to founder language (Freya + Leigh 24 Jul, Nathan 27 Jul). **This is the rubric for creating and grading all marketing fuel — creative, content, copy.**

**Emphasis:** Coverage (the connected loop) is the ownable spine; quiet forward-motion supports it, dialled down from the loud win/perform register (mostly Nathan) that was deliberately left out.

**The four:** **Personal** (you're not a number) · **Connected** (nothing falls through) · **Clear** (never confused) · **Ahead** (never caught out).

**PERSONAL** — *Freya: "treated as an individual, not a generic program."*
- **Is:** treats the person in front of it as an individual, never a generic case. Knows this clinician's practice and this client's pathway, and behaves like it. The opposite of one-size-fits-all software.
- **Design:** show the person, not just the data — name, history, pathway first; warm layouts over clinical dashboards; nobody feels like a record.
- **Copywriting:** write to one clinician about one client. "You"/"your client," never "users." Plain and specific; if a line could belong to any health-software company, rewrite it.
- **Content:** real AU allied-health situations, not generic "healthcare"; real clinicians and cases (with consent) over stock personas.
- **Customers:** remember each clinician's context and use it; personal check-ins over broadcasts.

**CONNECTED** — *Leigh: "link the patient, the care, the carer, the clinician all as one."*
- **Is:** one connected loop, not a stack of point solutions. Clinician has client, plan and record in one place, nothing dropped between sessions; the client (via Companion) stays connected to their clinician and their rehab, looked after not used.
- **Design:** everything joins on one screen, no flicking between tools; on Companion, tie the client to their clinician and plan, never alone with a generic app.
- **Copywriting:** talk about the whole loop and show both ends; "your client between visits," "one place," "nothing dropped." Not a scribe, not a plan tool — the thing that connects them.
- **Content:** the end-to-end story from both sides; contrast the disconnected stack where the client is handed a plan and left alone.
- **Customers:** close the loop with the clinician; make the client feel looked after, not processed.

**CLEAR** — *Leigh: "a sense of clarity, removal of any ambiguity."*
- **Is:** removes ambiguity — you always know the plan, where the client is, and what's next; no guesswork, no digging, no double entry.
- **Design:** one obvious next action per screen; plain labels, no clutter; scannable over dense.
- **Copywriting:** plain words a busy clinician gets in one read; short sentences, one idea at a time; if a line needs a second read, rewrite it.
- **Content:** explain, don't impress; make the complex simple and usable, with a takeaway they can act on today.
- **Customers:** straight answers, expectations set up front; never leave a clinician guessing where something stands.

**AHEAD** — *Leigh: "skate to where it's going." Freya: "I wanted to intervene immediately."*
- **Is:** keeps both sides a step ahead — the clinician sees what's coming, the client gets ahead in their own rehab. Never caught out, never catching up.
- **Design:** flag what needs attention before it's a problem; clinician sees the next step early, Companion flags off the client's wearables; forward prompts, not backward reports.
- **Copywriting:** active and forward; verbs that move (spot early, step in, stay ahead); no hype or hustle, keep the motion quiet.
- **Content:** point where the field is going; get clinicians ahead of shifting client expectations, regulation and tech before they have to react.
- **Customers:** proactive on both sides — flag before they ask, show the client they're moving forward.

## VOICE

**Signed by Mitch 2026-08-03.** Nothing carried over from pre-rebrand voice assets — this section is the voice source.

**Voice** — kitt sounds like a knowledgeable peer talking to one clinician about one client: plain, specific, forward, and quietly warm. Never a SaaS pitch. The voice is the four brand attributes applied to sentences:
- **Personal:** one clinician, one client; "you / your client", never "users"; specific, never generic.
- **Connected:** talk about the whole loop, show both ends.
- **Clear:** plain words a busy clinician gets in one read; short sentences, one idea at a time.
- **Ahead:** active, forward verbs; quiet motion, no hype or hustle.

**Tone** — the voice holds; tone is the voice made context-relevant by dialling the four attributes up or down. The rails never move: the Style & mechanics rules below are voice-level and cap every dial, so tone only ever operates inside them.

**The dials.** Four axes plus a POV switch. Each dial's cap is set by the rails, so it can't turn past the voice.

| Dial | Low ← → High | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| **Warmth** | Neutral, precise → Warm peer | Never cold/corporate; never gushing or chummy |
| **Formality** | Plain conversational → Formal, exact | Never stiff SaaS; clinical docs is the only formal end |
| **Density** | One action, terse → Expansive, teaching | Even "high" stays one-read per sentence |
| **Push** | Steady, explanatory → Active, forward | "High" is still quiet motion: never hype, never urgency |
| **POV (switch)** | Brand-neutral ↔ Founder first-person | Founder first-person only where a real person signs |

**Settings by context** — pick the row for the baseline dials:

| Context | Warmth | Formality | Density | Push | POV |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blog | Mid | Low–Mid | High | Mid | Brand / founder guest |
| Website | Mid | Mid | Mid | High | Brand |
| Social | High | Low | Low | Mid | Founder first-person |
| Clinical docs | Low | High | Mid | Low | Neutral |
| Email | Mid–High | Low–Mid | Low | Mid | Real person, signed |

**Style & mechanics**
- AU English throughout. No em-dashes. Lowercase-k **kitt**, always.
- "you / your client", never "users". "client" leads as the default; "patient" may be used but never interchangeably, one term per campaign/page/post, never mixed inside a single asset (see Terminology under ICPs BY MATURITY).
- One-read sentences, one idea at a time; active forward verbs, no hype.
- Specific over generic: real AU allied-health situations and numbers, not vague claims.
- No clinical-outcome claims: never claim kitt improves client outcomes.
- Not a scribe: never lead with note-taker framing; the note is one input to the connected loop.
- Frame the load forward: state the documentation and admin load as fact, then resolve it forward — kitt gives the clinician back the part of the job they trained for (treating and connecting). Never agitate it: no drowning, rescue, burnout or saviour framing — that's the banned move, and "stop drowning in admin" is the most generic line in the category.
- All published copy runs through ai-copywriter, not hand-written.

**Compliance signalling** *(final website wording still to be drafted via ai-copywriter and shipped by Mitch in the zoneblue repo)*
- The website carries a trust/compliance statement. The lane: kitt documentation **supports clinicians' AHPRA record-keeping obligations**, alongside the existing AU-hosting / APP-compliance claim.
- Never "AHPRA compliant", "AHPRA approved" or anything implying endorsement — AHPRA registers practitioners, not software, and the R4 ecosystem rule is compliance signalling only, never implied endorsement.
- Not "APRA" — APRA is the banking/insurance/super regulator and does not apply to kitt.
